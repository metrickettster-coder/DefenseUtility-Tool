The bundled clause and NIST SP 800-171 control datasets are illustrative, curated subsets — not a complete or authoritative restatement of FAR/DFARS or NIST SP 800-171. Verify against acquisition.gov, the DFARS PGI, and NIST SP 800-171 before relying on this for actual compliance determinations. Matching is keyword/ID-based, not semantic, and can both over- and under-match.
